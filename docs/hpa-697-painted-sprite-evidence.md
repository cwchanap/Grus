### In-world lance feasibility — measured (2026-11-21, review round 5)

An earlier revision of this note claimed closing the gap "would mean new or re-budgeted journeys, i.e. weakened gameplay/no-wave guards". That overstated the prohibition: appending steps to an existing real-input test — placements, digs, room builds, analyze, all through the real command/UI paths with every existing assertion untouched — is mechanically expressible and was genuinely attempted. The accurate, measured statement is narrower and is what actually blocks the capture:

- **Unlock horizon (irreducible):** the lance needs 4 500 points (50 % × 9 000). Best legal rates: 2.0/s heat-scaled (≈1.875/s at the 15/16 surface efficiency the defense buildings cause) plus 1.0/s per completed L3 Lab, where each Lab costs 150 Scrap after three sequential paid digs (30/45/60 s, 516 Scrap + 258 Coal). Crossing 4 500 under optimal play — 4 Labs plus a defended, gathering base — lands at match-time ≈1 250–1 500 s. Every existing journey ends by ≈620 s (defenses, mid-assault) or in a scripted Defeat + restart ≈500–600 s (combat lifecycle); the 300-s preparation guard caps the rest. The unlock is therefore time-infeasible inside any existing journey's horizon by a factor of ≈4–5, and it cannot be bought down: crystals→Analyze (90 points per 10 crystals) is bounded by kill rewards (1/2/5/50 per alien kind) and still needs the same surviving base.
- **What crossing the horizon costs:** the base must survive waves 2–13 (escalating 8+2N+N²/5 threat budgets, Behemoths from wave 10, two- and three-zone rotations from wave 10/20). A sim-side feasibility probe (ten design iterations, kept untracked at `crates/grus-sim/tests/lance_feasibility_probe.rs`; run `cargo test -p grus-sim --release --test lance_feasibility_probe -- --nocapture`) showed this requires: worker evacuation (the four seeded Workers stand inside the ring and idle/die there), a Depot built outside the ring to inherit deposits (workers otherwise kept routing to the Research Center and died commuting through the war lane), a ≈20-wall shell around the RC whose placement order is constrained by the reachable-immediate-perimeter builder-slot rule (a wrong order traps the builder inside a sealed pocket or starves a wall — both observed), re-routing the only near Coal source (it sits inside the wave lane; the assigned coal worker died commuting), and gap-aware builder scheduling with builder-death fallback (observed death mid-walk during a wave). The best partial run held waves 2–7 at full Research Center HP with an east plug + two funnel turrets but the economy collapsed in waves 4–5 (Scrap stockpile pinned at 5 while workers died), the plan starved, and wave 8 breached; research peaked at ≈1 794/4 500. Even the HP-placement half of the requested screenshot needs the lance to take damage, i.e. placement inside a live wave zone.
- **Why that stays a recorded gap rather than a fix in this task:** every individual step above is a legal real-input command — no sim/bridge state poke and no weakened guard is involved. But the aggregate is a new ≈500-line survival-strategy journey appended mid-combat to `defenses_smoke_test.gd` (the only plausible host, since it already runs through a wave with a wall ring and turret), plus multi-round windowed-pacing stabilization for a ≈1 200-s, 60-plus-interaction run at the ≈4.5 FPS renderer (for scale: the ≈300-s economy journey consumed two dedicated windowed fix rounds; defenses another one or two). That is a new journey in substance and a multi-round stabilization effort — both outside what the brief permits this task to do. The reviewer's alternative — a controller scope decision (either fund the long extension as its own reviewed round, or accept the non-in-world evidence set above for the replacement lance) — remains the open path, and this note now states the constraint without overclaiming a prohibition the brief does not make.
